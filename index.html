System overview

Automated Cloud Architecture & Data Flow

A scheduled, serverless pipeline runs a containerized handler, retrieves space-weather events from NASA DONKI, and streams execution details to CloudWatch.

STEP 1 · SCHEDULE

Amazon EventBridge

A rate(1 day) rule dispatches a scheduled event to the Lambda target.

STEP 2 · COMPUTE

AWS Lambda (container)

The Python 3.9 handler runs from the space-weather-repo ECR image in ap-south-2.

STEP 3 · INGESTION

NASA DONKI API

The pipeline requests coronal mass ejections (/DONKI/CME) and geomagnetic storms (/DONKI/GST).

STEP 4 · OBSERVABILITY

CloudWatch Logs

Execution output and API synchronization status are available in the dashboard log stream.

This dashboard simulates the Lambda test runner; it does not invoke AWS resources.

Space weather fundamentals

CMEs, geomagnetic storms & the Kp index

Coronal mass ejections (CMEs) are eruptions of magnetized solar material. When solar-wind conditions couple with Earth’s magnetosphere, they can cause geomagnetic storms. The planetary Kp index summarizes geomagnetic disturbance on a scale from 0 to 9; NOAA maps storm levels from G1 to G5.

CME · Solar material

Large clouds of magnetized plasma ejected from the Sun and observed by coronagraphs such as SOHO/LASCO.

GST · Magnetosphere

A disturbance in Earth’s magnetic environment driven by energy transferred from the solar wind.

Kp · Activity index

A planetary index of geomagnetic activity. NOAA storm categories begin at Kp 5 (G1).

NOAA geomagnetic storm scale

Approximate Kp threshold and typical impacts; effects vary by event and location.

G0 · QuietKp 0–4

No NOAA geomagnetic storm category; quiet to unsettled conditions.

G1 · MinorKp 5

Minor grid fluctuations; aurora may be visible at high latitudes.

G2 · ModerateKp 6

High-latitude power systems may experience alarms; satellite drag can increase.

G3 · StrongKp 7

Voltage corrections may be needed; radio navigation and HF communications can be affected.

G4 · SevereKp 8

Widespread voltage-control issues and satellite operations impacts are possible.

G5 · ExtremeKp 9

Severe impacts to power systems, satellite operations, and radio communications are possible.
