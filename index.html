import os, requests, json

def lambda_handler(event, context):
    api_key = os.environ.get("NASA_API_KEY", "DEMO_KEY")
    url = f"https://api.nasa.gov/DONKI/CME?api_key={api_key}"
    response = requests.get(url)
    data = response.json()
    return {
        "statusCode": 200,
        "body": json.dumps({"status": "synchronized", "events_count": len(data)})
    }

📡 Space Weather Fundamentals: CME, Flares & Geomagnetic Storms

Space weather phenomena originate from solar activity and propagate through the interplanetary medium. When Earth-directed plasma interacts with Earth's magnetosphere, it induces geomagnetic storms (GST) measured by the Kp index.

NOAA Geomagnetic Storm Severity (Kp Index) Scale

G1 (Minor) - Kp 5

Weak power grid fluctuations, minor impact on satellite operations, high-latitude aurora visible.

AURORA: Alaska, Canada
G2 - G3 (Moderate - Strong) - Kp 6-7

Voltage alarms at high-latitude power systems; satellite orientation changes may be required for drag.

AURORA: Northern US, Europe
G4 - G5 (Severe - Extreme) - Kp 8-9

Widespread voltage control problems; extensive surface charging on spacecraft; stunning low-latitude aurora.

AURORA: Global / Low Latitudes
